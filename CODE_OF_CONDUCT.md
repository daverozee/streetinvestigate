# Code of Conduct

We want Infinite Atlas to be a welcoming project for curious builders.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps the project:

- Being respectful and patient.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Welcoming newcomers and beginner questions.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Being mindful that people contribute from different backgrounds and time zones.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of discussions or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block participants whose behavior is harmful to the community.

If you see a problem, open a private conversation with the repository owner through GitHub.
